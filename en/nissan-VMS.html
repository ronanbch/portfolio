Nissan Vehicle Management Services

Publicis sapient
May - December 2019

Client context: This project was carried out as part of my role at Publicis Sapient, where I worked on behalf of Nissan Europe. It was built in parallel with the design of Nissan Europe's e-commerce platform.

My role: As UX designer, I had to study Nissan Europe's log tool and turn it into a back office for the e-commerce platform that was being built

    Organization: I worked under the Lead Designer, in close collaboration with the technical team.

    The approach


    I analyzed the existing back office for Nissan Europe's sites (mainly built around file search and log analysis) and reworked it to be more user-friendly, making room for a new type of user: dealerships.
